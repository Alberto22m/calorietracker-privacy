Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026.

The CalorieTracker app (the "app") is developed by Mane Hovakimyan. This policy describes what data the app processes.

1. Account and server

The app has no sign-up or account. The developer does not receive or store your data on any server.

2. Data you enter

Your food diary, products, dishes, weight, water, goals and profile settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer.

3. Apple Health

Apple Health integration is off by default. If you turn it on, the app can read and write nutrition and weight data in Health. This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer. You can revoke access in the app settings or in the Health section of your iPhone.

4. Camera and barcode scanner

The camera is used only to recognize barcodes. The image is not saved or transmitted. The scanned barcode number is sent to the open food database Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product name and ingredients.

5. Subscription

The subscription is purchased and paid through your Apple ID. The developer does not receive your card details or payment information. The app checks subscription status on your device through StoreKit.

6. Analytics and advertising

The app does not use analytics services, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

7. Your rights

To delete your data, delete the app: all data on the device is removed with it. You can cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: rnane.1@icloud.com.
