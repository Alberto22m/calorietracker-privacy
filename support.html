CalRing — Support

Questions, bug reports or feature ideas? Email us at rnane.1@icloud.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Subscription

Manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the app's subscription screen and tap “Restore purchases”.

Your data

CalRing has no account. Your food diary is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. See the Privacy Policy.

Data sources & attribution

Product and nutrition information in CalRing comes in part from Open Food Facts, a free, community-built food database. This data is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0; individual database contents are under the Database Contents License, and product images under CC BY-SA 3.0. Some nutrition values come from the USDA FoodData Central public-domain database.
